Questions Homeowners Ask

Get your free estimate
Is the estimate really free?
Yes. We come to your home, measure the space, check the subfloor and moisture, and send you a clear written estimate. There is no obligation.
Which areas do you serve?
The Kansas City metro on both sides of the state line, including Raymore, Lee's Summit, Belton, Overland Park and Olathe. If your town is not on the map below, call or text and ask.
Can you take out my old floors?
Yes. Removal of old flooring can be part of your project, so the subfloor is ready before the new floor goes in.
Do you supply the flooring, or can I buy my own?
Both work. We can quote material and installation together, or install flooring you already picked out.
Should I refinish my hardwood or replace it?
If the boards are solid and the damage is on the surface, refinishing usually brings them back for less than new floors. At the estimate we tell you plainly which one makes sense.
How long does an installation take?
It depends on the size of the space and the material. Your estimate includes a schedule, and we keep you updated every day of the job.
Do you install carpet or tile?
No. We focus on LVP, laminate and hardwood, plus refinishing, stairs and trim. Staying focused is how we keep the quality high.
