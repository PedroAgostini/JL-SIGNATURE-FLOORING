# Surface brief: index.html (one page)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: Kansas City metro homeowners arriving from Meta/Google ads, mostly on phones. Job: trust the installer, see the floors, request a free estimate (form, call or text). Proof on hand: logo, copy deck, 7+ years, owner-led crew; reviews and job photos still to come (placeholders and labeled stock). Constraints: no license, insurance, prices, address or hours; no carpet or tile; English; no em dashes.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is laid like the logo's parquet: a 45 degree plank grid of real floors set in black lacquer and gold. It refuses the category default of a full-bleed living room photo with a headline and a row of equal service cards.

OWN-WORLD: Warm black lacquer ground, gold leaf plank strips with black seams, white oak for the light passages. Strict ink set: black, gold, white oak and the wood in the photos; no neutral greys. Gloock display serif, Albert Sans text and wide tracked caps labels. Every service has one sample code (No. 01 to No. 06) reused in nav, 3D room, cards and form. Photos carry one fixed corner chip: room and material.

STORY: Visitors see the floors first, learn José's crew does six services around KC, explore them in a lit 3D room, compare materials, see work and process, then book a free estimate or call/text.

FIRST VIEWPORT (revised by the user on 2026-10-22): full-bleed photo of a finished hardwood room under a lacquer wash. Left: headline, sub and Call/Text. Right: the estimate form card (service chips 01 to 06, name, phone, ZIP, gold submit that sends the lead directly), framed by a fine gold line drawing of the logo diamond. The photo drifts slower than the page on scroll.

FORM: Parquet Grid, my top grounded candidate (pick card). seed: ca3b02087f5c1479. Signature interaction: Three.js cutaway house where choosing a service lights its part and the camera flies there (light is state).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
