## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS: one index.html plus an assets folder, no framework or build step. Hosting outside Wix (Netlify, Vercel or Hostinger), domain jlsignatureflooring.com. Lead form posts to Web3Forms (access key to be filled in by the agency).

## Users

Homeowners in the Kansas City metro (Raymore, MO and surrounding Kansas and Missouri cities) who want to replace worn, scratched or dated floors, add flooring to a remodel, or restore existing hardwood. Mostly 30 to 60 years old, 100% American clientele. They arrive from Meta Ads, Google Ads and the Google Business Profile, often on a phone, and they want to see real work, trust the installer, and get a free estimate with little friction. Preferred contact: Call and Text.

## Product Purpose

One page site for JL Signature Flooring, a residential flooring installer formalized in May 2025 by José Eduardo Fonseca Bastos, with 7+ years in the trade. The site exists to turn paid and local traffic into estimate requests (form, call, text). Success: steady, predictable leads that let José keep a crew busy and step back from doing every install himself.

## Positioning

A small, owner-led local crew: José still works on the floors. Clean job sites, precise subfloor preparation (moisture checked at inspection), durable finishes, clear estimates, timely completion, reliable daily communication.

## Operating Context

Lead flow: Lead → contact → qualification → estimate → site visit → proposal in Joist → follow-up → close → install → review. Estimates are free and on site. José brings floor samples to visits.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Services offered: LVP flooring, laminate flooring, solid hardwood installation, engineered hardwood installation, hardwood floor refinishing (sanding, restaining, clear coat), custom stairs (treads and risers), baseboards and trim (baseboard, quarter round, shoe molding), door installation, old floor demolition and removal.

Never offer: carpet, tile.

Priority for marketing: Hardwood, LVP, Refinishing (LVP and laminate for volume, hardwood for ticket, refinishing for high intent).

Undecided / pending (do not publish): street address and ZIP, license and liability insurance, prices, exact business hours (use "Call or text, 7 days a week"), service area radius and ZIP list, promotions.

## Product Principles

1. Every section ends one step from contact: Call, Text or the estimate form.
2. Show the floors. Proof through work, not adjectives.
3. Say only what is confirmed. Pending facts stay off the page.
4. Phone first: most visitors come from ads on mobile.
5. Speak like the owner on site: plain, specific, respectful.
